# Source article details

Open `new.html?test=1` in the LP5 preview and select the test project.

1. Confirm each article heading has a compact info button and the filename and
   keywords are hidden initially.
2. Click any info button. Confirm filenames and keywords appear inline for all
   articles, without an overlay, and every button changes to “Hide article
   details”.
3. Navigate between articles and use **Reload project**. Confirm details remain
   visible.
4. Click any info button again. Confirm filenames and keywords hide for all
   articles.
